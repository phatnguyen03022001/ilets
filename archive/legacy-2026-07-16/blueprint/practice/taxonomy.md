# Practice Taxonomy (v1.1)
*Canonical practice types ([practice-type-schema.md](practice-type-schema.md) v1.1), each classified against the canonical learning phases ([LD-006](../learning/decisions.md)): `primary_phase` + `phases`. Referenced by `id` from Skill Leaves + Curriculum Nodes.*

## Writing
**`PT-01` Scaffolded/guided writing** · primary: Acquisition · phases: [Acquisition, Consolidation] · mode: individual/adaptive · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: build a new writing ability with scaffolding that fades.
- cognitive_operations: apply, create. · supports: `W-*` acquisition (esp. W-GRA, W-CC, W-TA/TR). · reinforces: K-GRA, K-VOC.
- when_used: early in a band phase (after knowledge-first). · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate ([LD-003](../learning/decisions.md)).

**`PT-02` Sentence-combining drill** · primary: Acquisition · phases: [Acquisition, Fluency] · mode: individual · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: develop compound/complex sentence construction toward automaticity.
- cognitive_operations: apply. · supports: W-GRA-02, W-GRA-03, W-GRA-07. · reinforces: K-GRA-003/004/020/021.
- when_used: acquisition of sentence-structure leaves. · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate.

**`PT-03` Paraphrase task** · primary: Consolidation · phases: [Consolidation, Transfer] · mode: individual · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: reword prompts/data without repetition.
- cognitive_operations: create. · supports: W-LR-03, W-CC-04. · reinforces: K-VOC-020/041.
- when_used: consolidation. · feedback_model: AI-primary.

**`PT-04` Error-correction exercise** · primary: Consolidation · phases: [Consolidation, Retrieval] · mode: individual · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: identify and fix targeted errors.
- cognitive_operations: analyze, evaluate. · supports: W-GRA-*, W-LR-05. · reinforces: K-GRA, K-VOC-031.
- when_used: consolidation or diagnostic. · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate.

**`PT-05` Redraft-after-feedback** · primary: Transfer · phases: [Consolidation, Transfer] · mode: individual · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: improve a submission using feedback (redraft).
- cognitive_operations: create, evaluate. · supports: W-TA/TR/CC/LR/GRA (integrated). · reinforces: — .
- when_used: consolidation/transfer. · feedback_model: AI-primary, calibrated confidence ([LD-002](../learning/decisions.md)).

**`PT-06` Timed essay (Task 1/2)** · primary: Exam Readiness · phases: [Exam Readiness, Transfer] · mode: timed · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: produce a full task under exam conditions.
- cognitive_operations: create. · supports: all W-TA/W-TR + W-CC/LR/GRA (integrated). · reinforces: — .
- when_used: pre-exam / Exam-Preparation ([LD-005](../learning/decisions.md)). · feedback_model: AI-primary, post-task (delayed).

## Speaking
**`PT-07` Pronunciation drill (minimal pairs)** · primary: Acquisition · phases: [Acquisition, Retrieval] · mode: individual · applies_to: speaking
- objective_served: produce/contrast target phonemes.
- cognitive_operations: remember, apply. · supports: S-P-01, S-P-02. · reinforces: K-PHON-010/011/012.
- when_used: acquisition of pronunciation leaves. · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate (speech recognition).

**`PT-08` Shadowing** · primary: Fluency · phases: [Acquisition, Fluency] · mode: individual · applies_to: speaking
- objective_served: approximate rhythm, stress, connected speech toward automaticity.
- cognitive_operations: apply. · supports: S-P-03, S-P-04, S-FC-01. · reinforces: K-PHON-040/041.
- when_used: acquisition/consolidation. · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate.

**`PT-09` Long-turn practice (Part 2)** · primary: Consolidation · phases: [Consolidation, Transfer, Fluency] · mode: timed · applies_to: speaking
- objective_served: produce a coherent 1–2 min extended turn.
- cognitive_operations: create. · supports: S-FC-02, S-FC-04, S-GRA-02/03, S-LR-02. · reinforces: K-GRA-021, K-VOC-012.
- when_used: consolidation/transfer. · feedback_model: AI-primary, calibrated confidence.

**`PT-10` Q&A / role-play (Part 1/3)** · primary: Consolidation · phases: [Consolidation, Transfer] · mode: mixed · applies_to: speaking
- objective_served: respond to questions interactively.
- cognitive_operations: apply, create. · supports: S-FC-03/04/05, S-LR-01/04, S-GRA-04. · reinforces: K-VOC-012, K-GRA-010.
- when_used: consolidation. · feedback_model: AI-primary (conversational).

**`PT-11` Timed mock speaking** · primary: Exam Readiness · phases: [Exam Readiness] · mode: timed · applies_to: speaking
- objective_served: complete a full Speaking test under exam conditions.
- cognitive_operations: create. · supports: all S-* (integrated). · reinforces: — .
- when_used: pre-exam / Exam-Preparation. · feedback_model: AI-primary, post-task, calibrated confidence.

## Receptive (Listening / Reading)
**`PT-12` Skim/scan drill** · primary: Fluency · phases: [Acquisition, Fluency] · mode: timed · applies_to: listening, reading
- objective_served: locate gist/specific detail quickly (speed/automaticity).
- cognitive_operations: understand. · supports: L-COMP-01/02, R-COMP-01/02. · reinforces: K-VOC-010.
- when_used: acquisition. · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate.

**`PT-13` Comprehension question set** · primary: Consolidation · phases: [Consolidation, Retrieval] · mode: individual · applies_to: listening, reading
- objective_served: answer question types against a text/recording.
- cognitive_operations: understand, analyze. · supports: `*-COMP-*`, `*-QT-*`. · reinforces: K-VOC-011/012.
- when_used: consolidation. · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate (objective scoring).

**`PT-14` Note-taking from lecture/text** · primary: Transfer · phases: [Consolidation, Transfer] · mode: individual · applies_to: listening, reading
- objective_served: capture structure/key points of extended input.
- cognitive_operations: analyze. · supports: L-COMP-06, R-COMP-05/06. · reinforces: K-VOC-011.
- when_used: consolidation/transfer (Band 6+). · feedback_model: AI-primary.

**`PT-15` Timed section/passage practice** · primary: Exam Readiness · phases: [Exam Readiness, Fluency] · mode: timed · applies_to: listening, reading
- objective_served: complete a full section/passage under timing.
- cognitive_operations: understand, analyze, evaluate. · supports: `*-COMP-*`, `*-QT-*` (integrated). · reinforces: — .
- when_used: pre-exam / Exam-Preparation. · feedback_model: AI-primary, objective scoring.

**`PT-16` Distractor/error review** · primary: Retrieval · phases: [Retrieval, Consolidation] · mode: individual · applies_to: listening, reading
- objective_served: analyze why wrong options were wrong (re-engage material).
- cognitive_operations: evaluate. · supports: L-COMP-05, R-QT-02/03. · reinforces: — .
- when_used: diagnostic / remediation. · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate.

## Knowledge
**`PT-17` Spaced retrieval (flashcard)** · primary: Retrieval · phases: [Retrieval, Consolidation] · mode: adaptive · applies_to: knowledge, writing, speaking
- objective_served: reinforce retention via spaced retrieval.
- cognitive_operations: remember. · supports: knowledge-dependent leaves (W-LR, S-LR, W-GRA-05…). · reinforces: K-VOC-*, K-GRA-*.
- when_used: ongoing consolidation ([../learning/review.md](../learning/review.md) — performance-graded spacing). · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate.

**`PT-18` Collocation/word-formation drill** · primary: Acquisition · phases: [Acquisition, Consolidation] · mode: individual · applies_to: knowledge, writing
- objective_served: acquire collocations and word-formation patterns.
- cognitive_operations: remember, apply. · supports: W-LR-02, W-LR-05. · reinforces: K-VOC-020/030.
- when_used: acquisition. · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate.

**`PT-19` Gap-fill / completion** · primary: Consolidation · phases: [Consolidation, Retrieval] · mode: individual · applies_to: knowledge, listening, reading
- objective_served: reinforce form/accuracy within context.
- cognitive_operations: apply. · supports: W-LR-05, L-QT-01/05, R-QT-05. · reinforces: K-VOC-030/031, K-GRA-*.
- when_used: consolidation. · feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate.

## Cross-cutting / Exam
**`PT-20` Interleaved mixed set** · primary: Transfer · phases: [Transfer, Retrieval] · mode: mixed · applies_to: cross
- objective_served: discriminate across skills/topics via mixed practice.
- cognitive_operations: apply, analyze, evaluate. · supports: any (mixed). · reinforces: any (mixed).
- when_used: transfer phase (moderate interleaving). · feedback_model: AI-primary.

**`PT-21` Adaptive practice set** · primary: Consolidation · phases: [Consolidation, Retrieval, Transfer] · mode: adaptive · applies_to: cross
- objective_served: personalize practice selection/difficulty to learner state.
- cognitive_operations: (varies). · supports: any (selected by adaptation). · reinforces: any (selected).
- when_used: within-band adaptive sequencing ([LD-001](../learning/decisions.md)). · feedback_model: AI-primary, adaptive.

**`PT-22` Diagnostic checkpoint** · primary: Retrieval · phases: [Retrieval, Consolidation] · mode: diagnostic · applies_to: cross
- objective_served: diagnose current mastery across leaves/knowledge.
- cognitive_operations: (varies). · supports: any (scoped). · reinforces: — .
- when_used: band entry/exit, Exam-Preparation entry. · feedback_model: AI-primary, objective + calibrated on productive.

**`PT-23` Full mock test (multi-skill)** · primary: Exam Readiness · phases: [Exam Readiness] · mode: timed · applies_to: cross
- objective_served: simulate the full IELTS test (all four skills).
- cognitive_operations: (all). · supports: all leaves (integrated). · reinforces: — .
- when_used: Exam-Preparation ([LD-005](../learning/decisions.md)); does **not** certify progression. · feedback_model: AI-primary, post-test, calibrated confidence on Writing/Speaking.

## Phase coverage
Every canonical phase has ≥1 type with that `primary_phase`:
- **Acquisition** — PT-01, PT-02, PT-07, PT-18.
- **Consolidation** — PT-03, PT-04, PT-09, PT-10, PT-13, PT-19, PT-21.
- **Retrieval** — PT-16, PT-17, PT-22.
- **Transfer** — PT-05, PT-14, PT-20.
- **Fluency** — PT-08, PT-12.
- **Exam Readiness** — PT-06, PT-11, PT-15, PT-23.

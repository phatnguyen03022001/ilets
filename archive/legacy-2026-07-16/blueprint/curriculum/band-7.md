# Band 7 — Learning Pathway
*Schema v1.1. Sophistication band: structural flexibility + error-free frequency, less-common/idiomatic lexis, flexible cohesion with a clear developed position, reliable inference, and sustained pronunciation.*

**Entry:** Band 6 exit met. **Exit:** Band 7 exit criteria met ([../bands/](../bands/)).

---

**`C-B7-01` Structural flexibility & error-free frequency** · seq 1 · load: high · duration: 3–4h
- skill_leaves: `W-GRA-07`, `W-GRA-03` · knowledge_objects: `K-GRA-005`, `K-GRA-006`, `K-GRA-007`
- focus: a wide, flexible structural range with frequent error-free sentences.
- expected_outcomes: learner can produce varied, mostly error-free sentences.
- sequencing: refines Band 5/6 grammar (prereq W-GRA-03); cognitive create; knowledge-first (relative/conditional/noun clauses).
- exit: W-GRA-07 mastered; W-GRA-03 at Band 7.

**`C-B7-02` Lexical sophistication** · seq 2 · load: high · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `W-LR-04`, `S-LR-03` · knowledge_objects: `K-VOC-021`, `K-VOC-011`
- focus: less common, precise, and idiomatic vocabulary used accurately.
- expected_outcomes: learner can use less common/idiomatic items accurately and appropriately.
- sequencing: prereq Band 6 collocations; cognitive apply.
- exit: W-LR-04 mastered; S-LR-03 at Band 7.

**`C-B7-03` Cohesion, position & development mastery** · seq 3 · load: high · duration: 3–4h
- skill_leaves: `W-CC-03`, `W-CC-04`, `W-TR-02`, `W-TR-03` · knowledge_objects: `K-GRA-022`, `K-GRA-033`
- focus: flexible cohesion with a clear, fully developed position.
- expected_outcomes: learner can organize flexibly and sustain a clear, well-supported position.
- sequencing: refines Band 5/6; cognitive create.
- exit: those leaves at Band 7.

**`C-B7-04` Receptive — inference & structure mastery** · seq 4 · load: high · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `R-COMP-04`, `R-COMP-05`, `L-COMP-03`, `L-COMP-04` · knowledge_objects: `K-VOC-011`
- focus: reliable inference and text-structure understanding.
- expected_outcomes: learner can answer inferential and structure items reliably.
- sequencing: refines Band 5 receptive; cognitive analyze/evaluate.
- exit: those leaves at Band 7.

**`C-B7-05` Pronunciation — range & flexibility** · seq 5 · load: high · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `S-P-03`, `S-P-04` · knowledge_objects: `K-PHON-030`, `K-PHON-040`
- focus: sustained intonation, connected speech, and chunking.
- expected_outcomes: learner can sustain stress/intonation and chunking across long turns.
- sequencing: refines Band 5 pronunciation; cognitive apply.
- exit: S-P-03, S-P-04 at Band 7.

**`C-B7-06` Band 7 consolidation & exit** · seq 6 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: — · knowledge_objects: —
- focus: integrate Band 7 sophistication across skills.
- expected_outcomes: learner can perform across all four skills at Band 7 level.
- sequencing: mastery gate; precedes Band 8.
- exit: Band 7 exit criteria met for all four skills.

## Coverage check
Band 7 introduces structural flexibility (W-GRA-07) and less-common lexis (W-LR-04), and refines cohesion/position, receptive inference, and pronunciation to Band 7. Exits at Band 7 exit criteria.

# Band 4 — Learning Pathway
*Schema v1.1. First fully-structured band: consolidates Band 3 foundations to Band 4 competence and introduces Task analysis, paragraphing, sustained speech, and Band-4 receptive work.*

**Entry:** Band 3 exit met. **Exit:** Band 4 exit criteria met ([../bands/](../bands/)).

---

**`C-B4-01` Writing — accuracy, punctuation & paragraphing** · seq 1 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `W-GRA-01`, `W-GRA-06`, `W-CC-01` · knowledge_objects: —
- focus: accurate simple sentences, punctuation, and basic paragraphing.
- expected_outcomes: learner can write simple, punctuated, paragraphed text.
- sequencing: consolidates Band 3 (prereq); introduces paragraphing (W-CC-01).
- exit: W-GRA-01/06, W-CC-01 mastered.

**`C-B4-02` Writing task foundations — key features & prompt analysis** · seq 2 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `W-TA-01`, `W-TR-01` · knowledge_objects: —
- focus: identify key features (T1) and deconstruct the essay prompt (T2).
- expected_outcomes: learner can identify key features and analyse an essay prompt.
- sequencing: prereq Band 3 writing; cognitive analyze.
- exit: W-TA-01, W-TR-01 mastered.

**`C-B4-03` Vocabulary — topic sets & word choice** · seq 3 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `W-LR-01`, `S-LR-01`, `S-LR-04` · knowledge_objects: `K-VOC-012`, `K-VOC-040`
- focus: topic vocabulary and appropriate word choice.
- expected_outcomes: learner can discuss topics with adequate, appropriate vocabulary.
- sequencing: prereq C-B3-04 (core vocab); knowledge-first.
- exit: W-LR-01, S-LR-01, S-LR-04 mastered.

**`C-B4-04` Speaking — keeping going & basic accuracy** · seq 4 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `S-FC-01`, `S-GRA-04`, `S-P-05` · knowledge_objects: `K-GRA-010`
- focus: sustain speech, basic grammatical accuracy, and intelligibility.
- expected_outcomes: learner can speak continuously on familiar topics with clear meaning.
- sequencing: prereq Band 3 phonemes/sentences; consolidates to Band 4.
- exit: S-FC-01, S-GRA-04, S-P-05 mastered.

**`C-B4-05` Receptive — main ideas & details (Band 4 level)** · seq 5 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `L-COMP-01`, `L-COMP-02`, `R-COMP-01`, `R-COMP-02` · knowledge_objects: `K-VOC-012`
- focus: gist and specific detail in moderately complex texts.
- expected_outcomes: learner can identify main ideas and key details of moderately complex texts.
- sequencing: consolidates Band 3 receptive to Band 4.
- exit: the listed COMP leaves mastered.

**`C-B4-06` Basic receptive question types** · seq 6 · load: medium · duration: 2h
- skill_leaves: `L-QT-01`, `L-QT-05`, `R-QT-05` · knowledge_objects: —
- focus: completion and short-answer item types at Band 4.
- expected_outcomes: learner can complete basic receptive items within limits.
- sequencing: prereq C-B4-05; cognitive apply.
- exit: the listed QT leaves mastered.

**`C-B4-07` Band 4 consolidation & exit** · seq 7 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: — · knowledge_objects: —
- focus: integrate productive + receptive Band 4 abilities.
- expected_outcomes: learner can perform across all four skills at Band 4 level.
- sequencing: mastery gate — requires C-B4-01..06; precedes Band 5 ([LD-001](../learning/decisions.md)).
- exit: Band 4 exit criteria met for all four skills.

## Coverage check
Band 4 consolidates Band 3 foundations and introduces Task analysis (W-TA-01, W-TR-01), paragraphing (W-CC-01), sustained speech (S-FC-01), and Band-4 receptive work. Exits at Band 4 exit criteria.

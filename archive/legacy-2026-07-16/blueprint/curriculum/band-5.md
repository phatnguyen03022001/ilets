# Band 5 — Learning Pathway (schema v1.1, reference implementation)
*Sequenced curriculum nodes ([curriculum-node-schema.md](curriculum-node-schema.md) v1.1) referencing Skill Leaves + Knowledge Objects **by `id`** ([CR-001](decisions.md)). Band 5 is a dense mid-band: complex sentences, cohesion, Task 2 development, and receptive inference/distractors are introduced here.*

**Entry:** Band 4 exit criteria met. **Exit:** Band 5 exit criteria met ([../bands/](../bands/)), mastery-gated ([LD-001](../learning/decisions.md)).

---

**`C-B5-01` Knowledge foundation — expanding sentence structure** · seq 1 · load: high · duration: 3–4h
- skill_leaves: `W-GRA-02`, `W-GRA-03` · knowledge_objects: `K-GRA-003`, `K-GRA-020`, `K-GRA-004`, `K-GRA-021`
- focus: gain control of compound and complex sentences before applying them productively.
- expected_outcomes: learner can produce accurate compound and complex sentences in their own writing.
- sequencing: prereq-first (K-GRA-003/004 require K-GRA-002 from Band 4); knowledge-before-skill; band foundation.
- exit: W-GRA-02 & W-GRA-03 mastered; K-GRA-003/004/020/021 acquired.

**`C-B5-02` Cohesion & progression** · seq 2 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `W-CC-02`, `W-CC-03`, `W-CC-04` · knowledge_objects: `K-GRA-022`, `K-GRA-033`
- focus: organize ideas logically and link them with cohesive devices and reference.
- expected_outcomes: learner can structure a response with clear progression and flexible cohesion.
- sequencing: prereq W-CC-01 (Band 4); knowledge first; builds on C-B5-01.
- exit: W-CC-02/03/04 mastered; K-GRA-022/033 acquired.

**`C-B5-03` Writing Task 2 — position & development** · seq 3 · load: high · duration: 3–5h
- skill_leaves: `W-TR-02`, `W-TR-03`, `W-TR-04` · knowledge_objects: `K-VOC-012`
- focus: formulate a clear position, develop it with support, and maintain relevance.
- expected_outcomes: learner can write a Task 2 essay with a clear position and developed, relevant ideas.
- sequencing: prereq W-TR-01 (Band 4); applies C-B5-01/02; cognitive create.
- exit: W-TR-02/03/04 mastered.

**`C-B5-04` Writing Task 1 — overview & data reporting** · seq 4 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `W-TA-03` · knowledge_objects: `K-GRA-050`, `K-GRA-051`
- focus: select key features and report them with supporting data and appropriate tense.
- expected_outcomes: learner can write a Task 1 response reporting key features with accurate data.
- sequencing: prereq W-TA-01 (Band 4); applies C-B5-01; consolidates K-GRA-050/051.
- exit: W-TA-03 mastered.

**`C-B5-05` Lexical resource — paraphrase, word formation, accuracy** · seq 5 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `W-LR-03`, `W-LR-05` · knowledge_objects: `K-VOC-030`, `K-VOC-031`
- focus: paraphrase to avoid repetition; accurate word formation and spelling.
- expected_outcomes: learner can paraphrase effectively and produce accurately formed, correctly spelt text.
- sequencing: prereq W-LR-01 (Band 4); knowledge first; supports C-B5-03/04.
- exit: W-LR-03/05 mastered.

**`C-B5-06` Speaking — fluency, complex speech & pronunciation** · seq 6 · load: high · duration: 3–4h
- skill_leaves: `S-FC-02`, `S-FC-03`, `S-FC-04`, `S-FC-05`, `S-GRA-02`, `S-LR-02`, `S-P-02`, `S-P-03` · knowledge_objects: `K-GRA-021`, `K-VOC-012`
- focus: produce extended, coherent spoken turns with complex structures, paraphrase, and refined stress/intonation.
- expected_outcomes: learner can sustain a long turn with complex grammar, paraphrase, and controlled stress/intonation.
- sequencing: applies C-B5-01; prereq S-FC-01/S-P-01 (Band 4); cognitive create.
- exit: S-FC-02/03/04, S-GRA-02, S-LR-02, S-P-02/03 mastered.

**`C-B5-07` Receptive — inference, paraphrase, distractors** · seq 7 · load: high · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `L-COMP-03`, `L-COMP-04`, `L-COMP-05`, `R-COMP-03`, `R-COMP-04`, `R-COMP-05` · knowledge_objects: `K-VOC-011`
- focus: handle inference, paraphrased input, and distractors in listening and reading.
- expected_outcomes: learner can answer inferential items and reject distractors reliably.
- sequencing: prereq L-COMP-01/02, R-COMP-01/02 (Band 4); cognitive analyze/evaluate.
- exit: the listed COMP leaves mastered.

**`C-B5-08` Receptive — higher-order question types** · seq 8 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `L-QT-02`, `L-QT-03`, `L-QT-04`, `R-QT-01`, `R-QT-02`, `R-QT-04`, `R-QT-06` · knowledge_objects: —
- focus: apply strategies for higher-order listening/reading question types.
- expected_outcomes: learner can deploy question-type-specific strategies under timing pressure.
- sequencing: prereq C-B5-07 comprehension; cognitive apply/evaluate.
- exit: the listed QT leaves mastered.

**`C-B5-09` Band 5 consolidation & exit** · seq 9 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: — (integrates all Band 5 leaves) · knowledge_objects: —
- focus: integrate productive + receptive Band 5 abilities under exam conditions.
- expected_outcomes: learner can perform across all four skills at Band 5 level.
- sequencing: mastery gate — requires C-B5-01..08 exits; precedes Band 6 ([LD-001](../learning/decisions.md)).
- exit: Band 5 exit criteria met for all four skills ([../bands/](../bands/)), reliably demonstrated ([../learning/mastery.md](../learning/mastery.md)).

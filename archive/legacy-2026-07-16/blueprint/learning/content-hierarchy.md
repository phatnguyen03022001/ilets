# Content Hierarchy
*Element 9. Implements [philosophy.md](philosophy.md) #2 (teach exactly what's required) and #11 (clear objective + measurable outcome).*

## Purpose
Define how content is organized and prioritized.

## Evidence ([brief §9](learning-science-evidence-brief.md))
- **Foundational → advanced** progression is broadly supported (Bloom's taxonomy; learning progressions).
- **Grain size** and **micro-skill vs. whole-task** are `[OPEN]`; CLT suggests finer grain for novices (expertise reversal).

## Model
- **Foundational layers → task-specific strategies:** enabling knowledge (vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation — in `../knowledge/`) supports the four skills (`../skills/`), which support band-specific task strategies.
- *Proposed default (confirm):* **adaptive grain size** by band — decompose complex tasks into micro-skills for low bands; integrate to whole-task for high bands.
- Every content item maps to: a **learning objective**, a **measurable outcome**, a **band**, and a **skill/knowledge area** (Principle #11 + #2 — no over/under-teaching).

## Cautions
- Over-fragmentation impedes integration; premature integration yields fragile learning; isolated skills may not transfer.

## Dependencies
- Actual decomposition ← `../skills/` + `../knowledge/`; sequencing ← `../curriculum/`.

### Test format (official)
40 questions across 4 recordings (~30 min): Section 1 (social/everyday conversation), Section 2 (social/everyday monologue), Section 3 (education/training, conversation), Section 4 (academic monologue/lecture). Question types: multiple choice, matching, plan/map/diagram labelling, form/note/table/flow-chart completion, sentence completion, short-answer. Recordings are played once.

## 2. Evidence-Based Interpretation
*(Grounded in the official format above — interpretation, not a direct quote.)*
- Difficulty generally **increases across sections**: Sections 1–2 are social/everyday; Sections 3–4 are educational/academic, with Section 4 (extended academic lecture) the most demanding.
- The test rewards: distinguishing **main ideas vs specific detail**, following **signposting and paraphrase**, and managing **distractors** — all implied by the official question types.

## 3. Learning overlay (Bands 3–9) — Blueprint Inference
*Inference per [BD-003](decisions.md): official docs do not map receptive bands to comprehension abilities. The following translates the raw-score bands + test structure into learning prescriptions. **Confidence is stated per band.** Exit criteria cite the **Official Evidence** score range.*

### Band 3 — program entry point · *Confidence: Medium*
- **Required knowledge:** limited vocabulary for familiar, everyday topics.
- **Required skills:** catch isolated words/details in slow, predictable speech (mainly Section 1).
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** achieves the Band 3 raw-score range (~6–7/40) reliably → [Listening Skill rule](#listening-skill-exit-criteria-skill-level).
- **Acceptable residual errors:** comprehends only fragments; cannot follow extended speech.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** tracking main ideas across sections, academic content (Section 4), paraphrase — reserved for Band 4+.

### Band 4 · *Confidence: Medium*
- **Required knowledge:** vocabulary for familiar social contexts.
- **Required skills:** grasp the gist of simple social exchanges (Sections 1–2); frequent difficulty with educational/academic content (3–4).
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~10–12/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** misses much of Sections 3–4; struggles with paraphrase and distractors.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** sustained detail-tracking across all sections, academic-lecture comprehension — Band 5+.

### Band 5 · *Confidence: Medium*
- **Required knowledge:** everyday + some educational/training vocabulary.
- **Required skills:** follow the main points of familiar social and some educational content; locate specific details.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~16–18/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** misses detailed/abstract information; struggles with Section 4 detail and complex paraphrase.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** reliable academic-lecture comprehension, distractor management — Band 6+.

### Band 6 · *Confidence: Medium*
- **Required knowledge:** vocabulary across social and academic topics.
- **Required skills:** follow main ideas and specific details across most sections; handle common question types.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~23–25/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** some difficulty with dense/complex academic content (Section 4) and tricky distractors.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** reliable complex/abstract comprehension, near-full accuracy — Band 7+.

### Band 7 · *Confidence: Medium-High*
- **Required knowledge:** broad vocabulary including academic/abstract lexis.
- **Required skills:** follow extended speech reliably, including abstract/complex academic content; navigate paraphrase and most distractors.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~30–31/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** occasional misses on nuance or the most demanding detail.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** near-complete comprehension, minimal misses — Band 8+.

### Band 8 · *Confidence: Medium-High*
- **Required knowledge:** wide, precise vocabulary including idiomatic/academic items.
- **Required skills:** follow detailed, complex, abstract speech with little difficulty; manage distractors well.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~35–36/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** rare misses on subtle or dense detail.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** near-perfect comprehension — Band 9.

### Band 9 · *Confidence: Medium-High*
- **Required knowledge:** extensive, flexible vocabulary.
- **Required skills:** comprehend virtually all speech, including subtle/abstract/detailed content.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~39–40/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** an occasional single-item miss.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** none (ceiling band).

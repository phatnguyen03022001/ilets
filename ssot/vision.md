# Vision

## Mission
Trở thành nền tảng luyện thi IELTS số 1 thế giới

## Core Capabilities
1. **Listening** - Practice with authentic exam materials and instant feedback
2. **Reading** - Comprehension training with band-specific scoring
3. **Writing** - Rubric-strict feedback (TA/CC/LR/GRA) with redraft tracking
4. **Speaking** - Mock interviews with AI examiner and fluency analysis

## Entry Wedge: Writing (E)
- **Current Focus:** Rubric-strict feedback for Task 1/2
- **Value:** Per-criterion analysis + progression tracking
- **Moat:** What ChatGPT cannot do (stateless per chat; not consistently rubric-aligned; no progression history)

## Product Evolution Map
1. **Now:** Writing wedge (E) - 10 learners by 2026-08-31
2. **Phase 2:** Speaking + band-descriptor scoring
3. **Phase 3:** 4-skill mock test with cross-skill analytics
4. **Phase 4:** L1-interference targeting for specific markets
5. **Phase 5:** SRS corpus integration

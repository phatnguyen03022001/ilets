**Caveat (official):** exact boundaries vary by test version. ielts.org **average** anchors (authoritative): Band 5 = 15, Band 6 = 23, Band 7 = 30, Band 8 = 35. GT Reading requires **higher** raw scores for the same bands (official).

### Test format (official — Academic)
3 academic-sourced passages, 40 questions, 60 minutes. Passages increase in difficulty/complexity. Question types: multiple choice; identifying information (True/False/Not Given); identifying writer's views/claims (Yes/No/Not Given); matching information / headings / features / sentence endings; sentence / note / summary / diagram / table completion; short-answer.

## 2. Evidence-Based Interpretation
*(Grounded in the official format above — interpretation, not a direct quote.)*
- Passages become progressively **more demanding** (vocabulary, syntactic complexity, abstraction).
- The test rewards: **skimming/scanning** for gist and specific info, **detailed comprehension**, **inferencing** (including writer's views and purpose), and **distinguishing relevant from distractor** information — all implied by the official question types.

## 3. Learning overlay (Bands 3–9) — Blueprint Inference
*Inference per [BD-003](decisions.md): official docs do not map receptive bands to comprehension abilities. Translates raw-score bands + test structure into learning prescriptions. **Confidence stated per band.** Exit criteria cite the **Official Evidence** score range.*

### Band 3 — program entry point · *Confidence: Medium*
- **Required knowledge:** limited vocabulary.
- **Required skills:** locate isolated details in simple text (largely word-matching).
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** achieves the Band 3 raw-score range (~6–7/40) reliably → [Reading Skill rule](#reading-skill-exit-criteria-skill-level).
- **Acceptable residual errors:** cannot grasp main ideas; little beyond literal word-matching.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** gist, inference, academic vocabulary — Band 4+.

### Band 4 · *Confidence: Medium*
- **Required knowledge:** basic academic vocabulary.
- **Required skills:** locate specific information in accessible passages; grasp limited gist.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~10–12/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** struggles with dense/abstract passages; limited inference.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** inference, writer's views, complex matching — Band 5+.

### Band 5 · *Confidence: Medium*
- **Required knowledge:** everyday + some academic vocabulary.
- **Required skills:** identify main ideas and some details; handle simpler question types.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~15–18/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** misses abstract/inferential content; struggles with the most complex passage.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** reliable inference, writer purpose, dense academic text — Band 6+.

### Band 6 · *Confidence: Medium*
- **Required knowledge:** academic vocabulary across common topics.
- **Required skills:** understand main ideas and detailed information; manage most question types, including some inference.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~23–26/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** some difficulty with dense/abstract passages and nuanced inference.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** reliable complex inference, near-full comprehension — Band 7+.

### Band 7 · *Confidence: Medium-High*
- **Required knowledge:** broad academic vocabulary including abstract lexis.
- **Required skills:** understand extended/complex passages; handle inference, writer's views, and matching reliably.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~30–32/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** occasional misses on subtle or complex points.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** near-complete comprehension, minimal misses — Band 8+.

### Band 8 · *Confidence: Medium-High*
- **Required knowledge:** wide, precise vocabulary including idiomatic/academic items.
- **Required skills:** handle detailed, complex, abstract texts with little difficulty; manage distractors.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~35–36/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** rare misses on subtle argumentation.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** near-perfect comprehension — Band 9.

### Band 9 · *Confidence: Medium-High*
- **Required knowledge:** extensive, flexible vocabulary.
- **Required skills:** full comprehension, including subtle/abstract/detailed content.
- **Exit criteria (Official Evidence):** ~39–40/40 reliably.
- **Acceptable residual errors:** an occasional single-item miss.
- **Higher-band exclusions:** none (ceiling band).

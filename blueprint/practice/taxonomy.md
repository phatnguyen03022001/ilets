# Practice Taxonomy (v1.0)
*Canonical practice types ([practice-type-schema.md](practice-type-schema.md)), referenced by `id` from Skill Leaves + Curriculum Nodes. Not exercises — reusable templates ([PR-001](decisions.md)).*

## Writing
**`PT-01` Scaffolded/guided writing** · purpose: acquisition · mode: individual/adaptive · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: build a new writing ability with scaffolding that fades.
- cognitive_operations: apply, create.
- supports: `W-*` leaves in acquisition (esp. W-GRA, W-CC, W-TA/TR).
- reinforces: K-GRA, K-VOC.
- when_used: early in a band phase (after knowledge-first).
- feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate (acquisition; [LD-003](../learning/decisions.md)).

**`PT-02` Sentence-combining drill** · purpose: acquisition · mode: individual · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: develop compound/complex sentence construction.
- cognitive_operations: apply.
- supports: W-GRA-02, W-GRA-03, W-GRA-07.
- reinforces: K-GRA-003/004/020/021.
- when_used: acquisition of sentence-structure leaves.
- feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate.

**`PT-03` Paraphrase task** · purpose: consolidation · mode: individual · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: reword prompts/data without repetition.
- cognitive_operations: create.
- supports: W-LR-03, W-CC-04.
- reinforces: K-VOC-020/041.
- when_used: consolidation phase.
- feedback_model: AI-primary.

**`PT-04` Error-correction exercise** · purpose: consolidation/diagnostic · mode: individual · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: identify and fix targeted errors.
- cognitive_operations: analyze, evaluate.
- supports: W-GRA-*, W-LR-05.
- reinforces: K-GRA, K-VOC-031.
- when_used: consolidation or diagnostic.
- feedback_model: AI-primary, immediate.

**`PT-05` Redraft-after-feedback** · purpose: consolidation/transfer · mode: individual · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: improve a submission using feedback (redraft).
- cognitive_operations: create, evaluate.
- supports: W-TA/TR/CC/LR/GRA leaves (integrated).
- reinforces: — (applies prior knowledge).
- when_used: consolidation/transfer.
- feedback_model: AI-primary, calibrated confidence on productive feedback ([LD-002](../learning/decisions.md)).

**`PT-06` Timed essay (Task 1/2)** · purpose: exam_readiness · mode: timed · applies_to: writing
- objective_served: produce a full task under exam conditions.
- cognitive_operations: create.
- supports: all W-TA/W-TR + W-CC/LR/GRA (integrated).
- reinforces: — .
- when_used: pre-exam / Exam-Preparation mode ([LD-005](../learning/decisions.md)).
- feedback_model: AI-primary, post-task (delayed, exam-realistic).

## Coverage
- **Productive** (Writing/Speaking) → PT-01..11. **Receptive** → PT-12..16. **Knowledge** → PT-17..19. **Cross-cutting/Exam** → PT-20..23.

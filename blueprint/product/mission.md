# Mission & Vision

> **Status:** Salvaged from the prior direction and reframed per the L1-agnostic decision ([FD-002](foundational-decisions.md)). **Wording requires Founder confirmation — see PRODUCT-001.**

## Mission (canonical, English)
Become the world's leading IELTS learning platform.

## Vision
A single, evidence-based Blueprint that completely defines how a learner progresses from Band 3 to Band 9 across Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking — precise enough to implement without further learning-system decisions, and rigorous enough that no learner is over-taught or under-taught.

## Notes
- The mission was originally articulated in Vietnamese (*"Trở thành nền tảng luyện thi IELTS số 1 thế giới"*). Per [FD-002](foundational-decisions.md) (L1-agnostic), English is canonical and the Vietnamese articulation belongs in `localization/vi/`, not here.

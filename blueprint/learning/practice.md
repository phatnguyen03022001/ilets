# Practice Model
*Element 5. Implements [philosophy.md](philosophy.md) #5 (deliberate practice), #10 (cognitive load), and [LD-003](decisions.md) (stage-dependent feedback).*

## Purpose
Define how practice is designed across skills and bands.

## Evidence-based mechanisms ([brief §3](learning-science-evidence-brief.md))
- **Retrieval practice** (Roediger & Karpicke 2006; Adesope et al. 2017): retrieving > restudying.
- **Spacing / distributed practice** (Cepeda et al. 2008): spaced > massed; gap ≈ 10–20% of retention interval.
- **Interleaving** (Dunlosky et al. 2013): mixed > blocked for transfer.
- **Worked examples / scaffolding** (Sweller et al. 2019): reduce load for novices; **expertise reversal** — fade as skill grows.
- **Deliberate practice** (Ericsson 1993): focused, goal-oriented, feedback-driven — **quality over volume**. The "hours" framing is rejected; practice explains limited variance (Macnamara 2016).

## Proposed defaults (confirm during review)
- **Grain size:** adaptive by band — fine micro-skill decomposition for low bands; coarser whole-task for high bands (expertise reversal).
- **Interleaving:** moderate — mix within a skill; cross-skill review.
- **Scaffolding:** gradual fading — worked examples → independent practice as expertise develops.
- **Difficulty:** adaptive within a band (per [LD-001](decisions.md)).

## IELTS application
- Spaced retrieval of vocabulary/grammar; interleave task types; worked exemplars for Writing Task 2 fading to independent writing; reduce scaffolding for Band 7+.

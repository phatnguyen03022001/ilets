# Learning Philosophy

The canonical set of pedagogical principles that govern the entire Blueprint. Every subsequent section (`bands/`, `skills/`, `knowledge/`, `curriculum/`, `practice/`, `assessment/`, `progress/`) must implement and remain consistent with these.

> **Status:** Principles are **Founder-decided** (2026-07-15). Evidence alignment from the audited [learning-science-evidence-brief.md](learning-science-evidence-brief.md). Principle/evidence conflicts were surfaced per the Founder meta-rule; the two genuine conflicts (#7 timing, #4 prerequisites) are **resolved** by [LD-003](decisions.md) and [LD-004](decisions.md). Two others (#5, #10) needed only clarification.

## Founder meta-rule
Research leads every major pedagogical decision. The principles below are honored **wherever evidence supports them**. If research conflicts with a principle: present the evidence, explain the trade-offs, and **ask the Founder** instead of choosing automatically.

## Principles & evidence alignment
Legend: ✅ supported · ◑ supported with nuance/clarification · ❓ limited/indirect evidence.

| # | Principle | Alignment | Evidence (source in [brief](learning-science-evidence-brief.md)) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Learning is the product.** | ✅ (framing) | Criterion-referenced mastery (Bloom 1968; Black & Wiliam 1998). |
| 2 | **Teach exactly what is required — no over/under-teaching.** | ✅ (framing) | Criterion-referenced assessment; practice quality over volume (Ericsson 1993; Macnamara 2016). |
| 3 | **Mastery-based progression.** | ✅ | Mastery learning (Bloom 1968; Kulik et al. 1990). |
| 4 | **Strong prerequisite enforcement.** | ✅ (resolved) | → [LD-004](decisions.md): Required/Recommended/Independent; minimum hard gates. Evidence is `[OPEN]`, hence the soft classification. |
| 5 | **Deliberate practice over passive consumption.** | ◑ (clarified) | Supported (Ericsson 1993); the "hours volume" form is rejected (Macnamara 2016). |
| 6 | **Frequent formative assessment.** | ✅ | Formative assessment (Black & Wiliam 1998; Sortwell 2024). |
| 7 | **Immediate, actionable feedback.** | ✅ (resolved) | → [LD-003](decisions.md): stage-dependent timing (acquisition immediate; transfer delayed/retrieval-first). |
| 8 | **AI augments, never replaces thinking.** | ✅ | "Assistance dilemma"; ITS ≈ human, over-reliance erodes metacognition (VanLehn 2011). |
| 9 | **Long-term retention via evidence-based review.** | ✅ | Spaced retrieval / expanding intervals (Cepeda 2008; Murre & Dros 2015). |
| 10 | **Minimize unnecessary cognitive load.** | ◑ (clarified) | Extraneous-load reduction (Sweller 2019); "desirable difficulties" preserved via the "unnecessary" qualifier. |
| 11 | **Every activity has a clear objective and measurable outcome.** | ✅ (framing) | Criterion-referenced goals; feed-up feedback (Hattie & Timperley 2007). |

## Resolved conflicts
- **#7 (timing) → [LD-003](decisions.md):** stage-dependent feedback timing. Acquisition (Band 3–5) = immediate; consolidation/transfer (Band 6–9) = increasingly delayed / retrieval-first. Implemented in [assessment.md](assessment.md).
- **#4 (prerequisites) → [LD-004](decisions.md):** Required / Recommended / Independent with minimum hard gates; each hard prerequisite requires an evidence-based rationale. Implemented in [prerequisite.md](prerequisite.md).

## Clarifications (no conflict; documented)
- **#5 (deliberate practice):** means focused, feedback-driven *quality* practice, not accumulated hours. Implemented in [practice.md](practice.md).
- **#10 (cognitive load):** cut *extraneous* load; preserve *generative/productive* load (retrieval, interleaving). Implemented in [practice.md](practice.md).

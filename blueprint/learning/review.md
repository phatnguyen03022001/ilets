# Review Philosophy
*Element 7. Implements [philosophy.md](philosophy.md) #9 (evidence-based review).*

## Purpose
Define how long-term retention is maintained.

## Evidence ([brief §7](learning-science-evidence-brief.md))
- **Forgetting curve** (Ebbinghaus 1885; Murre & Dros 2015): rapid initial loss; **expanding retrieval** restores retention.
- **Spacing + retrieval** jointly beat massed restudy for consolidation / relearning.

## Model
- **Expanding-interval** review of vocabulary, grammar, and task strategies.
- *Proposed default (confirm):* **performance-graded spacing** — schedule the next review when retrieval accuracy drops toward ~80–90% (i.e., just before it is fully lost), rather than a fixed calendar.

## IELTS application
- Vocabulary/grammar on expanding intervals; mix immediate and delayed review of task strategies.

## Cautions
- Intervals too long or too short both reduce effectiveness; forgetting rates vary by learner and material (supports adaptive scheduling per [LD-001](decisions.md)).

- Review scheduling rules ← `../progress/` (adaptive within band) and `../practice/`.

# Band 3 — Learning Pathway (program entry / foundation)
*Schema v1.1. Band 3 is the program **entry point** ([BD-001](../bands/decisions.md)): acquire core knowledge and develop basic communicative ability to reach Band 4 readiness.*

**Entry:** learner is at/above Band 3 (below Band 3 = outside the structured range; see [../bands/](../bands/)). **Exit:** Band 3 exit criteria met — can convey basic meaning across skills.

---

**`C-B3-01` Foundations — word classes, clause & simple sentence** · seq 1 · load: medium · duration: 3–4h
- skill_leaves: `W-GRA-01`, `S-GRA-01` · knowledge_objects: `K-GRA-010`, `K-GRA-001`, `K-GRA-002`
- focus: build the grammatical core — word classes and the simple sentence.
- expected_outcomes: learner can form accurate simple sentences (written and spoken).
- sequencing: knowledge-first (K-GRA-010→001→002); foundation of the entire program.
- exit: W-GRA-01 & S-GRA-01 mastered (entry level); K-GRA-010/001/002 acquired.

**`C-B3-02` Nouns, countability & basic determiners** · seq 2 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `W-GRA-05` · knowledge_objects: `K-GRA-061`, `K-GRA-032`, `K-GRA-040`
- focus: handle nouns, countability, and basic articles/determiners.
- expected_outcomes: learner can use basic determiners and articles without severely impeding meaning.
- sequencing: prereq C-B3-01; knowledge-first.
- exit: W-GRA-05 mastered (entry level); K-GRA-061/032/040 acquired.

**`C-B3-03` Basic tense & agreement** · seq 3 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `W-GRA-04` · knowledge_objects: `K-GRA-050`, `K-GRA-051`, `K-GRA-060`
- focus: present/past simple and subject–verb agreement.
- expected_outcomes: learner can use basic tenses accurately in simple sentences.
- sequencing: prereq C-B3-01; knowledge-first.
- exit: W-GRA-04 mastered (entry level); K-GRA-050/051/060 acquired.

**`C-B3-04` Core vocabulary & spelling** · seq 4 · load: medium · duration: 3–4h
- skill_leaves: `W-LR-01`, `W-LR-05`, `S-LR-01` · knowledge_objects: `K-VOC-010`, `K-VOC-031`
- focus: high-frequency vocabulary and basic spelling accuracy.
- expected_outcomes: learner can use common vocabulary to convey basic meaning with legible spelling.
- sequencing: parallel to grammar nodes; knowledge-first; load balanced.
- exit: W-LR-01/05, S-LR-01 mastered (entry level); K-VOC-010/031 acquired.

**`C-B3-05` Phoneme foundations & intelligibility** · seq 5 · load: high · duration: 3–4h
- skill_leaves: `S-P-01`, `S-P-05` · knowledge_objects: `K-PHON-010`, `K-PHON-011`, `K-PHON-012`
- focus: English phoneme inventory and minimal intelligibility.
- expected_outcomes: learner can produce individual phonemes clearly enough to be broadly understood.
- sequencing: knowledge-first (K-PHON-010/011 before contrasts); foundational for all speaking.
- exit: S-P-01/05 mastered (entry level); K-PHON-010/011/012 acquired.

**`C-B3-06` Receptive basics — gist & detail** · seq 6 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `L-COMP-01`, `L-COMP-02`, `R-COMP-01`, `R-COMP-02` · knowledge_objects: `K-VOC-010`
- focus: understand main ideas/gist and locate specific details.
- expected_outcomes: learner can identify the gist and key details of simple spoken/written texts.
- sequencing: applies C-B3-04 vocabulary; foundational comprehension.
- exit: the listed COMP leaves mastered (entry level).

**`C-B3-07` Basic receptive question types** · seq 7 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: `L-QT-01`, `L-QT-05`, `R-QT-05` · knowledge_objects: —
- focus: completion and short-answer item types.
- expected_outcomes: learner can complete basic receptive items within limits.
- sequencing: prereq C-B3-06 comprehension; cognitive apply.
- exit: the listed QT leaves mastered (entry level).

**`C-B3-08` Band 3 consolidation & exit** · seq 8 · load: medium · duration: 2–3h
- skill_leaves: — (integrates Band 3 foundations) · knowledge_objects: —
- focus: integrate foundational grammar, vocabulary, phonology, and comprehension to convey basic meaning.
- expected_outcomes: learner can communicate at a basic level across all four skills.
- sequencing: mastery gate — requires C-B3-01..07 exits; precedes Band 4.
- exit: Band 3 exit criteria met (entry certification; [../bands/](../bands/)).

---

## Coverage check
Band 3 establishes the core knowledge (word classes, clause/simple sentence, basic tense/agreement, core vocabulary, phonemes) and entry-level skill across all four skills. Foundational leaves are introduced here and refined Band 4+.

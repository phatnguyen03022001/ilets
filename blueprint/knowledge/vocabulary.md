# Vocabulary — Knowledge Graph (`K-VOC`, schema v1.1)
*Atomic knowledge objects with explicit edges + `examples` / `common_misconceptions`. Skill-leaf resolution in [resolution.md](resolution.md).*

## Core lexis
**`K-VOC-010` High-frequency / core vocabulary** — vocabulary · bands 3–9
- definition: the most frequent English word families (general-service vocabulary) needed for basic communication.
- requires: — .
- related_to: K-VOC-011, K-VOC-012.
- examples: *approach, area, assess, consist, derive*.
- common_misconceptions: confusing similar high-frequency words (e.g., *effect/affect*); wrong word class (*success/successful*).

**`K-VOC-011` Academic vocabulary** — vocabulary · bands 5–9
- definition: formal academic word stock (e.g., AWL families) for academic reading/writing.
- requires: K-VOC-010.
- related_to: K-VOC-040.
- examples: *analyze, concept, establish, framework, significant*.
- common_misconceptions: overusing "big words" without precision; register-inappropriate academic lexis in speech.

**`K-VOC-012` Topic-specific word sets** — vocabulary · bands 4–9
- definition: vocabulary organized by common IELTS topics (environment, technology, education, health, urbanization, etc.).
- requires: K-VOC-010.
- related_to: K-VOC-020.
- examples: *emissions, renewable, curriculum, infrastructure* (topic-clustered).
- common_misconceptions: memorizing lists without usage; topic-word miscollocation.

## Word partnership
**`K-VOC-020` Collocations** — vocabulary · bands 6–9
- definition: recurrent word partnerships (verb+noun, adj+noun, noun+noun).
- requires: K-VOC-010.
- related_to: K-VOC-021.
- examples: *make a decision, heavy rain, draw a conclusion, pose a threat*.
- common_misconceptions: word-by-word L1 translation (*do a decision*); assuming any synonym fits the same partner.

**`K-VOC-021` Idioms & fixed expressions** — vocabulary · bands 7–9
- definition: idiomatic/chunked expressions and their appropriate use.
- requires: K-VOC-010.
- related_to: K-VOC-020.
- examples: *a double-edged sword, to strike a balance*.
- common_misconceptions: forcing idioms inappropriately; literal-translation of idioms; register misuse.

## Form & accuracy
**`K-VOC-030` Word formation / affixation** — vocabulary · bands 5–9
- definition: prefixes/suffixes and word-family derivation (noun↔verb↔adj↔adv).
- requires: K-VOC-010.
- related_to: K-GRA-010.
- examples: *produce → production → productive → productively*.
- common_misconceptions: wrong suffix for the needed word class; spelling shifts under affixation (*occur → occurrence*).

**`K-VOC-031` Spelling rules & patterns** — vocabulary · bands 3–9
- definition: English spelling conventions and high-error patterns.
- requires: K-VOC-010.
- related_to: K-VOC-030.
- examples: *ie/ei rules; doubling consonants; silent letters*.
- common_misconceptions: *"definately"; "accomodate"*; phonetic-spelling assumption.

## Use & variation
**`K-VOC-040` Register & formality** — vocabulary · bands 5–9
- definition: choosing vocabulary appropriate to register (academic/formal vs informal/neutral).
- requires: K-VOC-010.
- related_to: K-VOC-011.
- examples: *children (neutral) vs kids (informal); obtain (formal) vs get*.
- common_misconceptions: informal lexis in academic writing; inconsistent register.

**`K-VOC-041` Synonymy & paraphrase resources** — vocabulary · bands 5–9
- definition: synonym sets, connotation, and paraphrase resources for varying expression.
- requires: K-VOC-010.
- related_to: K-VOC-020.
- examples: *important: crucial, significant, vital, key*.
- common_misconceptions: treating synonyms as fully interchangeable (ignoring collocation/connotation/register).

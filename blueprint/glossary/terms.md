# Glossary

Canonical terminology for the Blueprint. Every term is defined **once** here; other documents link rather than redefine. Implementation/technology terms (frameworks, databases, auth methods) are intentionally excluded — this glossary is learning/IELTS domain only.

> **Status:** Seeded from the prior direction. Terms will be expanded and evidence-cited as sections are authored.

## IELTS assessment
- **Band Score** — The 0–9 scale used to report IELTS proficiency (official). Half-band increments (e.g. 6.5) are reported for overall and some section scores. *(Full band descriptors: to be specified in `../bands/`.)*
- **Academic / General Training (variant)** — The two IELTS module variants. Listening and Speaking are shared; Reading passages and Writing Task 1 differ. See [FD-001](../product/foundational-decisions.md).
- **Task 1 / Task 2** — The two Writing tasks. Task 2 (essay) is shared across variants; Task 1 differs (Academic: describe visual data; GT: write a letter).
- **TA — Task Achievement** — Writing assessment criterion (Task 1 & 2). *(Detailed in `../assessment/`.)*
- **CC — Coherence and Cohesion** — Writing assessment criterion.
- **LR — Lexical Resource** — Writing and Speaking assessment criterion.
- **GRA — Grammatical Range and Accuracy** — Writing and Speaking assessment criterion.

## Learning model
- **Skill** — A first-class IELTS skill: Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking. See [FD-003](../product/foundational-decisions.md).
- **Knowledge layer** — Enabling knowledge (Vocabulary, Grammar, etc.) that supports all skills. Not itself scored.
- **Micro-skill** — A specific sub-skill within a skill or knowledge area (e.g. identifying paragraph headings in Reading; using articles in Grammar).
- **Learning method** — A strategy for acquiring/practicing content (e.g. spaced repetition, retrieval practice). *(Detailed in `../learning/`.)*
- **Mastery** — Demonstrated readiness to progress (evidence-based). *(Detailed in `../progress/`.)*

## Practice & feedback
- **Practice item** — A single exercisable unit (to be typed in `../practice/`).
- **Submission** — A learner's produced response submitted for evaluation (esp. Writing/Speaking).
- **Feedback** — Evaluation output: strengths, weaknesses, scores, and actionable improvements.
- **Redraft** — A revised version of a submission produced after feedback.

## Structure
- **Canonical** — The English, L1-agnostic source of truth. See [FD-002](../product/foundational-decisions.md).
- **Localization** — An L1-specific guidance layer mapped onto canonical without changing requirements.
- **Variant** — Academic or General Training.

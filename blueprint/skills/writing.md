# Writing — Skill Decomposition

## W-TA — Task Achievement (Task 1)

**W-TA-01 — Identify key features in visual data**
- Objective: identify the most important features (main trends, differences, stages, comparisons) in a graph/chart/table/diagram/map.
- Mastery: reliably identifies the 3–5 key features from a novel visual, matching an expert key.
- Prerequisites: — (basic graph/numeracy literacy assumed).
- Bands: 4–9.
- Traces to: Task 1 TA — Band 4 "few key features selected" → Band 8 "key features skilfully selected".

**W-TA-02 — Write an overview**
- Objective: write a clear overview statement summarizing the main trends/differences/stages, distinct from detail.
- Mastery: produces an accurate overview distinct from supporting detail in ≥2 Task 1 responses.
- Prerequisites: W-TA-01.
- Bands: 6–9.
- Traces to: Task 1 TA — Band 6 "a relevant overview is attempted" → Band 7 "presents a clear overview".

**W-TA-03 — Report key features with supporting data**
- Objective: select relevant key features and support them with accurate figures/data, avoiding irrelevant/inaccurate detail.
- Mastery: supports key features with correct data across ≥2 responses; no large irrelevant/inaccurate sections.
- Prerequisites: W-TA-01.
- Bands: 5–9.
- Traces to: Task 1 TA — Band 6 "supported using figures/data" → Band 7 "covered and clearly highlighted".

## W-TR — Task Response (Task 2)

**W-TR-01 — Analyse the prompt**
- Objective: identify all parts of the essay prompt and the required task type (opinion / discussion / advantages-disadvantages / problem-solution).
- Mastery: correctly identifies all prompt parts and task type across ≥3 novel prompts.
- Prerequisites: — .
- Bands: 4–9.
- Traces to: Task 2 TR — Band 4 "misunderstanding of the prompt" → Band 6 "main parts of the prompt are addressed".

**W-TR-02 — Formulate a clear position**
- Objective: formulate and state a clear position that directly answers the question.
- Mastery: states a clear, relevant position sustained throughout in ≥2 responses.
- Prerequisites: W-TR-01.
- Bands: 5–9.
- Traces to: Task 2 TR — Band 5 "expresses a position, but development unclear" → Band 7 "clear and developed position".

**W-TR-03 — Develop main ideas with support**
- Objective: extend each main idea with relevant reasons, examples, and evidence.
- Mastery: each main idea extended with relevant, sufficient support in ≥2 responses.
- Prerequisites: W-TR-02.
- Bands: 5–9.
- Traces to: Task 2 TR — Band 6 "main ideas relevant, but… insufficiently developed" → Band 7 "main ideas extended and supported".

**W-TR-04 — Maintain relevance**
- Objective: keep all content relevant to the prompt; avoid irrelevant or repetitive material.
- Mastery: no large irrelevant/repetitive sections across ≥2 responses.
- Prerequisites: W-TR-01.
- Bands: 5–9.
- Traces to: Task 2 TR — Band 5 "may be irrelevant detail" → Band 7 "lack of focus and precision in supporting ideas".

## W-CC — Coherence & Cohesion

**W-CC-01 — Paragraphing**
- Objective: organise writing into logical paragraphs, each with a clear topic.
- Mastery: uses appropriate paragraphing consistently (T2: intro/body/conclusion; T1: logical grouping).
- Prerequisites: — .
- Bands: 4–9.
- Traces to: CC — Band 4 "may be no paragraphing" → Band 7 "paragraphing generally used effectively".

**W-CC-02 — Overall logical progression**
- Objective: arrange information/ideas in a logical sequence with clear overall progression.
- Mastery: response shows clear logical progression throughout.
- Prerequisites: W-CC-01.
- Bands: 5–9.
- Traces to: CC — Band 5 "lack of overall progression" → Band 7 "clear progression throughout".

**W-CC-03 — Cohesive devices (linkers/conjunctions)**
- Objective: use a range of cohesive devices accurately, without over/under-use or mechanical patterns.
- Mastery: uses cohesive devices flexibly and accurately; no mechanical/overused linking.
- Prerequisites: `K-GRA` conjunctions; W-CC-02.
- Bands: 5–9.
- Traces to: CC — Band 5 "limited/overuse of cohesive devices" → Band 7 "used flexibly".

**W-CC-04 — Reference & substitution**
- Objective: use reference (pronouns, determiners) and substitution to link ideas without repetition.
- Mastery: uses reference/substitution to avoid repetition with minimal error.
- Prerequisites: `K-GRA` reference; W-CC-02.
- Bands: 6–9.
- Traces to: CC — Band 6 "reference may lack flexibility" → Band 7 "reference and substitution used flexibly".

## W-LR — Lexical Resource

**W-LR-01 — Topic-specific vocabulary**
- Objective: use adequate, relevant vocabulary for the task topic.
- Mastery: uses sufficient topic-appropriate vocabulary; meaning clear.
- Prerequisites: `K-VOC` topic word lists.
- Bands: 4–9.
- Traces to: LR — Band 4 "vocabulary is basic" → Band 6 "generally adequate and appropriate".

**W-LR-02 — Collocation**
- Objective: use accurate collocations (word partnerships).
- Mastery: uses collocations accurately; few inappropriate pairings.
- Prerequisites: W-LR-01; `K-VOC` collocations.
- Bands: 6–9.
- Traces to: LR — Band 7 "awareness of style and collocation" → Band 8 "despite occasional inaccuracies in… collocation".

**W-LR-03 — Paraphrase**
- Objective: paraphrase to avoid repetition and to reword prompts/data.
- Mastery: paraphrases successfully throughout; minimal verbatim copying of the prompt.
- Prerequisites: W-LR-01.
- Bands: 5–9.
- Traces to: LR/CC — avoidance of repetition (Band 6+ resource adequacy).

**W-LR-04 — Less common / precise / idiomatic vocabulary**
- Objective: use less common, precise, and (appropriately) idiomatic vocabulary.
- Mastery: uses some less common/idiomatic items accurately.
- Prerequisites: W-LR-01.
- Bands: 7–9.
- Traces to: LR — Band 7 "some ability to use less common and/or idiomatic items" → Band 8 "skilful use".

**W-LR-05 — Spelling & word formation**
- Objective: spell accurately and form words correctly.
- Mastery: few spelling/word-formation errors; none that impede communication.
- Prerequisites: `K-VOC`.
- Bands: 4–9.
- Traces to: LR — spelling/word-formation across bands 4–9.

## W-GRA — Grammatical Range & Accuracy

**W-GRA-01 — Simple sentence accuracy**
- Objective: produce accurate simple sentences.
- Mastery: simple sentences largely error-free.
- Prerequisites: `K-GRA` basic sentence structure.
- Bands: 4–9.
- Traces to: GRA — Band 4 "simple sentences predominate" → Band 9 full control.

**W-GRA-02 — Compound sentences**
- Objective: produce accurate compound sentences (coordination).
- Mastery: uses compound sentences correctly.
- Prerequisites: W-GRA-01; `K-GRA` coordination.
- Bands: 5–9.
- Traces to: GRA — sentence-form range.

**W-GRA-03 — Complex sentences (subordination)**
- Objective: produce a variety of accurate complex sentences (relative clauses, conditionals, concession, noun clauses, etc.).
- Mastery: uses complex structures with reasonable accuracy; variety evident.
- Prerequisites: W-GRA-01; `K-GRA` subordination.
- Bands: 5–9.
- Traces to: GRA — Band 5 "complex sentences attempted but faulty" → Band 7 "a variety of complex structures".

**W-GRA-04 — Tense & aspect**
- Objective: use appropriate tense and aspect accurately.
- Mastery: tense/aspect appropriate and accurate.
- Prerequisites: `K-GRA` tense/aspect.
- Bands: 4–9.
- Traces to: GRA — grammatical accuracy.

**W-GRA-05 — Articles & determiners**
- Objective: use articles and determiners accurately.
- Mastery: article/determiner errors do not impede meaning.
- Prerequisites: `K-GRA` articles/determiners.
- Bands: 4–9.
- Traces to: GRA — grammatical accuracy.

**W-GRA-06 — Punctuation**
- Objective: punctuate accurately.
- Mastery: punctuation generally well-controlled; rarely impedes communication.
- Prerequisites: — .
- Bands: 4–9.
- Traces to: GRA — punctuation across bands.

**W-GRA-07 — Structural flexibility / variety**
- Objective: use a wide, flexible range of structures.
- Mastery: uses varied structures flexibly; majority of sentences error-free.
- Prerequisites: W-GRA-02, W-GRA-03.
- Bands: 7–9.
- Traces to: GRA — Band 7 "a variety of complex structures… some flexibility" → Band 9 "full flexibility and control".

# Listening — Skill Decomposition (schema v1.1)
*Decomposes the Listening competency from [../bands/listening.md](../bands/listening.md). Receptive skills have **no public analytic descriptors** (band = raw-score conversion), so this decomposition is **Blueprint Inference** ([BD-003](../bands/decisions.md)): leaves are comprehension abilities + question-type strategies, each traced to the receptive band range and the official test format. Conforms to [leaf-schema.md](leaf-schema.md) v1.1.*

## Hierarchy overview
```
Listening
├─ L-COMP  Comprehension abilities
│   ├─ L-COMP-01  Understand main ideas / gist
│   ├─ L-COMP-02  Understand specific details
│   ├─ L-COMP-03  Understand purpose / attitude / opinion
│   ├─ L-COMP-04  Recognize paraphrase & signposting
│   ├─ L-COMP-05  Manage distractors
│   └─ L-COMP-06  Follow extended/complex academic speech (Section 4)
└─ L-QT    Question-type strategies
    ├─ L-QT-01  Form/note/table/flow-chart completion
    ├─ L-QT-02  Multiple choice
    ├─ L-QT-03  Matching
    ├─ L-QT-04  Map/plan/diagram labelling
    └─ L-QT-05  Sentence completion & short-answer
```

## L-COMP — Comprehension abilities

**`L-COMP-01` Understand main ideas / gist** — listening · COMP · bands 4–9 · cognitive: understand · load: medium
- objective: identify the main idea/gist of an extended spoken text.
- traces_to: Listening receptive overlay ([../bands/listening.md](../bands/listening.md)); Blueprint Inference.
- prerequisites: — .
- mastery_criteria: identifies the main idea of a Section 1–3 recording reliably.
- common_errors: focusing on detail over gist; missing the overall point.
- remediation: gist-listening drills; summarize-in-one-sentence tasks.
- consumer fields: _(populated by practice/ & assessment/; dependents derived)_.
- independence: ✅ atomic; teachable/practiceable/assessable/remediable.

**`L-COMP-02` Understand specific details** — listening · COMP · bands 4–9 · cognitive: understand · load: medium
- objective: identify specific factual details (numbers, names, times, places).
- traces_to: receptive overlay; Blueprint Inference.
- prerequisites: — .
- mastery_criteria: captures targeted details accurately from a recording.
- common_errors: number/spelling confusion; missing detail at speed.
- remediation: detail-capture dictation; number/name spelling drills.
- consumer fields: _(TBD / derived)_.
- independence: ✅ atomic; teachable/practiceable/assessable/remediable.

**`L-COMP-03` Understand purpose / attitude / opinion** — listening · COMP · bands 5–9 · cognitive: analyze · load: high
- objective: infer speaker purpose, attitude, or opinion from tone and language.
- traces_to: receptive overlay; Blueprint Inference.
- prerequisites: L-COMP-01.
- mastery_criteria: identifies speaker attitude/opinion reliably across items.
- common_errors: literal interpretation; missing modal/tonal cues.
- remediation: tone-cue spotting; attitude-question practice.
- consumer fields: _(TBD / derived)_.
- independence: ✅ atomic; teachable/practiceable/assessable/remediable.

**`L-COMP-04` Recognize paraphrase & signposting** — listening · COMP · bands 5–9 · cognitive: understand · load: high
- objective: recognize when the recording paraphrases the question/options (answer is reworded, not verbatim).
- traces_to: receptive overlay; Blueprint Inference.
- prerequisites: L-COMP-01.
- mastery_criteria: matches reworded answers to questions reliably.
- common_errors: expecting verbatim matches; missing synonyms/paraphrase.
- remediation: paraphrase-awareness drills; synonym mapping.
- consumer fields: _(TBD / derived)_.
- independence: ✅ atomic; teachable/practiceable/assessable/remediable.

**`L-COMP-05` Manage distractors** — listening · COMP · bands 5–9 · cognitive: evaluate · load: high
- objective: reject plausible-but-wrong options (distractors).
- traces_to: receptive overlay; Blueprint Inference.
- prerequisites: L-COMP-02.
- mastery_criteria: selects correct options over distractors reliably.
- common_errors: falling for partial-correct distractors; answering too early.
- remediation: distractor-analysis; "why wrong" justification drills.
- consumer fields: _(TBD / derived)_.
- independence: ✅ atomic; teachable/practiceable/assessable/remediable.

**`L-COMP-06` Follow extended/complex academic speech (Section 4)** — listening · COMP · bands 6–9 · cognitive: understand · load: high
- objective: follow a dense, extended academic monologue (Section 4).
- traces_to: receptive overlay — Band 6 "some difficulty with dense/complex academic content" → Band 7 "follow extended speech reliably".
- prerequisites: L-COMP-01, L-COMP-04.
- mastery_criteria: follows a Section-4 lecture, answering detail/main-idea items reliably.
- common_errors: cognitive overload; losing the thread; missing abstract points.
- remediation: note-taking from lectures; scaffolded Section-4 practice.
- consumer fields: _(TBD / derived)_.
- independence: ✅ atomic; teachable/practiceable/assessable/remediable.

## L-QT — Question-type strategies

**`L-QT-01` Form/note/table/flow-chart completion** — listening · QT · bands 4–9 · cognitive: apply · load: medium
- objective: complete gaps using words from the recording, respecting word limits & form.
- traces_to: official Listening format; receptive overlay.
- prerequisites: L-COMP-02.
- mastery_criteria: completes such items accurately, within word limits, with correct spelling.
- common_errors: exceeding word limit; wrong word form; spelling errors.
- remediation: word-limit drills; form-aware completion practice.
- consumer fields: _(TBD / derived)_.
- independence: ✅ atomic; teachable/practiceable/assessable/remediable.

**`L-QT-02` Multiple choice** — listening · QT · bands 5–9 · cognitive: apply · load: medium
- objective: select correct single/multiple answers from options.
- traces_to: official Listening format; receptive overlay.
- prerequisites: L-COMP-01, L-COMP-05.
- mastery_criteria: selects correct options reliably, including multi-answer variants.
- common_errors: single-answer mindset on multi-answer items; distractor traps.
- remediation: question-type-specific strategy; option-elimination drills.
- consumer fields: _(TBD / derived)_.
- independence: ✅ atomic; teachable/practiceable/assessable/remediable.

**`L-QT-03` Matching** — listening · QT · bands 5–9 · cognitive: apply · load: medium
- objective: match items (e.g., items to speakers/options) across the recording.
- traces_to: official Listening format; receptive overlay.
- prerequisites: L-COMP-01.
- mastery_criteria: matches items accurately across a section.
- common_errors: one-to-one bias; losing track across speakers.
- remediation: matching-strategy drills; speaker-tracking practice.
- consumer fields: _(TBD / derived)_.
- independence: ✅ atomic; teachable/practiceable/assessable/remediable.

**`L-QT-04` Map/plan/diagram labelling** — listening · QT · bands 5–9 · cognitive: apply · load: medium

- mastery_criteria: labels visuals accurately following directions.
- common_errors: spatial-orientation errors; missing directional cues.
- remediation: spatial-language pre-teaching; map-labelling drills.
- consumer fields: _(TBD / derived)_.
- independence: ✅ atomic; teachable/practiceable/assessable/remediable.

**`L-QT-05` Sentence completion & short-answer** — listening · QT · bands 4–9 · cognitive: apply · load: medium
